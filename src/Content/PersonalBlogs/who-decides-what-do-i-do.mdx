lately, I am not quite myself.

I feel like I might die as a mediocre, because I never in my life have done any extraordinary things. And when I try to do it I already give up because I never saw things actually happening with myself.
I see people everyday in my office or anywhere, and there are people I get jealous of. It feels wrong that I can't feel one good thing about people. These days people aren't inspiring me in any ways, they are just making me miserable and I don't want to blame them because we came across each other at wrong time. And my mind isn't accepting that people are way ahead of me, talented, extraordinary, curious and all sorts of good things.

Sometimes Its hard to make sense of everything. I wish I never had the sense to make sense of everything, at least not this early.

I don't know but my brain seems slow these days like a jam wheel trying hard to rotate. My mood is almost dependent on others, the way they behave with me, the way they talk with me.

And again the urge to make sense outweighs my knowledge. Inside my brain there are threads tangled with each other, when I pull one of them it gets more jammed.

I feel controlled in a way where no one is involved other than me and my thoughts. One might say my thoughts are quite normal, or they are not really ambitious, or they are just thoughts nothing cool about them and sadly I accept that.

Like I don't understand, why do we even care about what others think? but eventually we ask for gratification and validation from others. You know what I really have in my mind to say but I am just figuring out how do I articulate it. The world is running by the people living in it and the people are so messed up that they have created paradox in every single thing. When people say you don't have to care about them, then how the world is even suppose to run like I'd not care about my family, friends or anyone close to me, and because we are tied in this dependency of people we can't escape but to care about people, what they think, their thoughts and everything.

I think the wisdom is that, start accepting that you are ultimately dependent on people, you can't just go around and say fuck off on their face. But yeah, surely we aren't completely dependent on others.

let's say If I was completely dependent on myself, and I'd say fuck off to everyone, now the case is I want to reach home, I will not take any sorts of transports, I will have to use ground to walk on because roads are made by the people. I can't walk under the lights I won't have any access to artificial lights unless I have my own ancient torch. I can't stay in the house which isn't build by myself and later I also realise that I am naked because I don't have my own clothes but lets say somehow I managed to wear something but the big realisation is that I happen to have travel back in ancient time(?) and I think from here the civilisation starts.

Yet it's another tangled thread in my mind.
