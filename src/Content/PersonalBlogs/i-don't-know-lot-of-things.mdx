<Verse>
I don't know how to write, the way I want to write, 
though I imagine myself writing the way I want to write,
but that carries no meaning if it's just imagination.

I don't know how to draw, 
though my hands have been swirling on paper since childhood.
Yet, it's difficult to draw a straight line.  

I don't even know how to continue writing this, 
how to beat my lazy ass mind.
</Verse>
